The group's lore

The group was formed through the survival show R U Next?, a collaboration between HYBE and JTBC. They were known as I’LL-IT as pre-debut. They were initially supposed to debut as a 6-member group, but Youngseo, a pre-debut member, left before the group officially debuted. Youngseo is now a group called ALLDAY PROJECT. R U Next? aired for 10 episodes from June 30, 2023 until September 1, 2023. During the finale's broadcast, six contestants were chosen for the debuting lineup of the show's product group, I'LL-IT. Illit made their debut on March 25, 2024 with the mini album Super Real Me.

Illit's DEBUT

ILLIT's debut mini album, Super Real Me, along with its title track "Magnetic", was released on March 25, 2024. According to Hanteo Chart, Super Real Me sold 380,056 copies in its first week, breaking the then-record for the highest first-week sales of a girl group's debut album. On April 2, the group achieved their first-ever music show win on The Show for "Magnetic". ILLIT's fandom name, GLLIT (글릿), was formally announced on September 1, 2024. According to Belift Lab, 'GLLIT' was inspired by the English word "glitter", which means to sparkle and shine. The name means in ILLIT's eyes, their fans shine brightest. Moreover, the fans make ILLIT sparkle.
